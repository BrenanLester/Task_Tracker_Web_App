App Overview

DebugMyDay is your all-in-one digital workspace designed for peak efficiency. It allows you to seamlessly organize, manage, and track daily tasks, professional projects, and long-term goals. Our clean, intuitive interface is built to maximize your productivity while ensuring your personal data remains private and secure at all times.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower every individual to take control of their time and turn aspirations into measurable accomplishments. We provide an exceptionally intuitive, reliable, and secure task management platform that simplifies complex planning.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be the most trusted and widely-used personal and professional task management platform globally, recognized for transforming user productivity and helping millions achieve their fullest potential through structured daily planning.

About Us

DebugMyDay originated as a collaborative project by Group 1 of BSIS 2 at La Verdad Christian College. It was developed specifically for the Responsive Web Design and Web Application Development course to apply our theoretical knowledge in creating a secure, efficient, and real-world task management platform.
